# Development Instructions

- Always perform development work directly on the `main` branch.
- Push completed work directly to `origin/main`; do not create development or feature branches.
- Before every push, obtain explicit confirmation for the exact repository, remote, and branch, as required by the repository's global agent instructions.

## Basic App Usage

1. Launch LifeLook and complete the two-step setup.
2. Give the household a name and add every person whose income, spending, or goals belong in the financial plan. Birth dates are optional and can be typed as `MM/DD/YYYY` or selected with the calendar button.
3. Add at least one financial account. Select its type, enter a recognizable name, and provide its current balance. Enter money owed on a credit card as a negative balance.
4. Finish setup to open the local workspace.
5. Use **Overview** for the financial summary, **Activity** for transactions, **Plan** for projections, **Net Worth** for accounts and assets, and **Settings** to manage household members and preferences.
6. Add or update household members from **Settings** and save the changes when finished.
7. LifeLook stores financial data locally on the device and does not create an online account. Keep a separate protected backup of important data.

LifeLook is pre-release planning software. Do not use it as the only financial record or treat its projections and tax estimates as financial or tax advice.
